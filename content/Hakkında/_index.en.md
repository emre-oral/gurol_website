## About 

I completed my primary, middle and high school education at TED Karabük College. 
After graduating from Dokuz Eylül University Faculty of Medicine in 1990, I completed my residency in Otorhinolaryngology (Ear, Nose and Throat) at Istanbul Haseki Training and Research Hospital, where I then worked as a specialist for four years. 

After three years as a specialist and Chief Physician at Siirt State Hospital, I was appointed to Bodrum in 2003. 

I served at Bodrum State Hospital for twenty years. Since 2022, I have been seeing patients at my own private practice.
